# Lewis & Clark — Corps of Discovery

An open-world expedition RPG following the 1804–1806 Corps of Discovery. The player leads the Corps up the Missouri and across to the Pacific, where history sets the destination and landmarks but the player's choices decide how the journey actually unfolds.

## Language

### The Corps

**Corps**:
The whole expedition party the player is responsible for.
_Avoid_: party, team, crew

**Corps Strength**:
The headcount of the unnamed members of the Corps, tracked as a single number.
_Avoid_: troops, men

**Companion**:
A named historical member of the Corps (Clark, York, Drouillard, Sacagawea, …) with their own health, Conditions, and Ability.
_Avoid_: special character, hero, NPC

**Leader**:
The Companion the player directly controls in the world; the rest of the Corps follows.
_Avoid_: player character, avatar

**Condition**:
A lasting state on a Companion — wounded, sick, frostbitten, exhausted — that changes what they can do until treated or recovered.
_Avoid_: status effect, debuff

**Ability**:
A Companion's signature action, usable once and then recharged by Resting.
_Avoid_: power, skill, special

**Rest**:
Making camp; it passes time, recharges Abilities, and is where the game can be saved.
_Avoid_: sleep, camp action

**Corps Skill**:
One of the Corps' shared competencies — Hunting, Diplomacy, Navigation, Medicine, Survival — that improves through use.
_Avoid_: stat, attribute, level

### The world

**Region**:
A hand-built, freely explorable stretch of the historical route containing its Landmarks.
_Avoid_: level, zone, map

**Side Area**:
A generated, optional area branching off a Region, different each playthrough.
_Avoid_: dungeon, random map

**Landmark**:
A fixed historical place within a Region where a historical Scenario occurs.
_Avoid_: waypoint, POI

**Expedition Map**:
The strategic overview of the whole route, used to plan and undertake Legs and to review Discoveries.
_Avoid_: hex map, world map, overworld

**Landing**:
The spot at the upstream end of a Region where the Corps sets out on a Leg.
_Avoid_: exit, dock, travel point

**Leg**:
A journey between two Regions taken on the Expedition Map, costing days or weeks on the Calendar.
_Avoid_: fast travel, trip

**Day Clock**:
Time of day as it passes in real time while the Leader is in a Region.
_Avoid_: game time

**Calendar**:
The historical date of the expedition, which drives seasons.
_Avoid_: day clock, turn

**Winter Quarters**:
A Region where the Corps can safely see out the winter, such as the Mandan Villages; setting out from one into winter means wintering there first.
_Avoid_: safe zone, fort

**Winter Lock**:
Being caught by winter before reaching safe ground, forcing the Corps to halt until spring at great cost.
_Avoid_: freeze, winter penalty

### Stories and events

**Scenario**:
A branching, choice-driven story moment, whether historical, random, or a consequence of an earlier choice.
_Avoid_: event, dialogue, quest

**Random Event**:
A Scenario that fires by chance, weighted by Region, season, time of day, and the state of the Corps.
_Avoid_: random encounter

**Encounter**:
Something visible in the world the Leader can approach or avoid — wildlife, a trader, a hunting party.
_Avoid_: mob, spawn

**Chain**:
A Scenario planted by an earlier choice that fires some days later.
_Avoid_: trigger, follow-up, quest line

**Skill Check**:
A Scenario choice whose success chance is shown to the player before it is rolled, set by Corps Skills and which Companions are present.
_Avoid_: dice roll, saving throw

**Stand-off**:
A Scenario of high tension between the Corps and another people, resolved through choices rather than combat.
_Avoid_: battle, fight, combat

**Discovery**:
A plant, animal, or geographic feature documented in the Journal, which grants a lasting benefit.
_Avoid_: collectible, specimen

**Journal**:
The expedition's running record of Discoveries and events, ending with a comparison to what really happened.
_Avoid_: log, codex

### Peoples

**Nation**:
A Native people the Corps meets, referred to by its real name.
_Avoid_: tribe (in design and player-facing text), faction, enemy

**Reputation**:
How a single Nation regards the Corps; changes partly carry over to that Nation's neighbours.
_Avoid_: faction standing, hostility

### Outcomes

**Ending**:
How an expedition concludes — reaching the Pacific and returning, or failing through collapsed Corps Strength, mutiny, a lost season, or the Leader's death.
_Avoid_: game over, win/lose screen

**Ironman**:
An optional mode with a single save that cannot be reloaded.
_Avoid_: hardcore, permadeath mode

**Vertical Slice**:
The single playable Region (Council Bluffs → Teton Sioux, Aug–Sept 1804) built to prove the game is fun before committing to an engine and art direction.
_Avoid_: demo, prototype, MVP
