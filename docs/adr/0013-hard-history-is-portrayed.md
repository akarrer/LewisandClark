# The hard parts of the expedition are portrayed, not softened

York was enslaved by Clark, and Clark refused him his freedom for years after the expedition. Sacagawea was a Shoshone captive of the Hidatsa, married to Charbonneau, and about sixteen. The captains held courts-martial and ordered men flogged. The game shows all of it: York's status acknowledged and his skill and agency shown on their own terms; Sacagawea from her real roles — one link in a chain of interpreters, a recogniser of landmarks, calm when the pirogue nearly capsized — not the guide of the popular myth; and discipline as the captain's own decision, with the historical sentence as the norm of its time, shown by its aftermath and never graphically. A grounded, reverent game that omitted these would be the erasure ADR-0004 was written against.

## Consequences

The paid cultural and sensitivity review that ADR-0004 makes a release requirement is extended to cover York's and Sacagawea's portrayal, with Shoshone and Hidatsa sources included alongside the journals.
