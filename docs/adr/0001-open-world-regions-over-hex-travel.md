# Open world of Regions replaces hex-by-hex travel

The game began as a hex-map strategy game; we're turning it into an open-world RPG aimed at a public release, because walking the land with the Corps is where the player agency and discovery live. The world is a set of hand-built Regions along the historical route plus generated Side Areas, not one seamless map — a compressed 4,000-mile continuous world would be empty and beyond what we can build. The hex map survives as the Expedition Map, used only for Legs between Regions.

## Considered Options

- **Seamless world** — rejected: scale and emptiness.
- **Hex map stays the core, field scenes as vignettes** — rejected: keeps the strategy layer but caps the RPG ambition.
- **Both as parallel ways to travel** — rejected: two travel systems means balancing everything twice, and walking to a waypoint double-counted progress.

## Consequences

Time splits in two: a real-time Day Clock inside Regions and Calendar jumps on Legs. Everything previously counted in hexes is re-anchored — Ability cooldowns recharge on Rest, Chains wait in-game days, and Winter Lock becomes a Calendar check when departing on a Leg.
