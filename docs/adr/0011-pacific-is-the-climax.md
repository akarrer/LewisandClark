# Five Acts to the Pacific; the return is an Epilogue

The journey is played in Acts from Council Bluff to the Pacific — up the Missouri, Fort Mandan, the Upper Missouri, the crossing, and the Pacific with Fort Clatsop — and the journey home is an Epilogue, told rather than played in full and shaped by the state the expedition reached the Pacific in. The Pacific is the dramatic peak, and a second full journey back would cost as much as the first for less. Winter Quarters are played, compressed, because winter is where the simulation (ADR-0010) matters most and where the Corps' most important relationships were formed.

## Consequences

- Two Medicine, the expedition's only killing, is not the playable Stand-off ADR-0004 once named it as. It is told honestly in the Epilogue, through Lewis's journal, and changed by the Corps' Reputation with the Nations of the upper country. It is never skipped.
- "Reaching the Pacific" replaces "reaching the Pacific and returning" as the successful Ending.
