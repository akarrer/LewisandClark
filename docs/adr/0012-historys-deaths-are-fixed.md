# History's own deaths are fixed

Amends ADR-0003. Soft history lets Companions die who did not, but it does not let the player save one who did. Sergeant Floyd dies on 20 August 1804 whatever the player does: it was almost certainly a burst appendix, which nothing in 1804 could have cured, and a Skill Check that saved him would be false agency that undercuts the Journal's closing comparison with what really happened. The player's agency is in how the Corps meets the death — the burial, the naming of Floyd's Bluff, the election that fills his place.
