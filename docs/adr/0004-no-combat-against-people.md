# No combat against people; real Nations, individually respected

Only wildlife can be fought (hunting and grizzly charges). Tense meetings with Native peoples — the Teton Sioux at Bad River, the Blackfeet at Two Medicine — are Stand-offs resolved through Scenario choices and Skill Checks, never action combat. Nations appear under their real names with historical figures, and Reputation is tracked per Nation rather than labelling anyone "hostile". Framing Nations as enemies would be historically wrong and would sink a public release; generic fictional tribes would erase the history the game is about.

## Consequences

A paid cultural/sensitivity review of Nation portrayal is a release requirement and must be budgeted in the release plan. Sources include tribal histories, not only the expedition journals.

Amended by ADR-0011 (Two Medicine is told in the Epilogue, not played as a Stand-off) and ADR-0013 (the review also covers York and Sacagawea).
