# Soft history: fixed landmarks, divergent outcomes

The start, the goal, and the major Landmarks follow the real expedition, but outcomes are the player's: Companions can die, Nations can turn against the Corps, and the expedition can fail. We rejected a rails version (choices only move resources) because it removes stakes, and a full sandbox because it stops being about this expedition. The Journal ends by comparing the player's expedition with what really happened — that comparison is how history is taught without forcing it.

Amended by ADR-0012: history's own deaths are fixed; the agency is in how the Corps meets them.
